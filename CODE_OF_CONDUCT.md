# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in IN2NEXT Nexus.

Participants are expected to contribute in a professional and respectful manner regardless of background, experience level, role, or technical discipline.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Welcoming newcomers.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Discussing ideas rather than attacking people.
- Acknowledging different perspectives.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Helping others learn.
- Respecting project and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption.
- Sexual harassment.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or sustained bad-faith behavior.
- Malicious impersonation.
- Other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Technical Disagreement

Technical disagreement is expected and healthy.

Disagreements should focus on:

- Evidence
- Requirements
- Trade-offs
- Technical constraints
- Project goals
- User impact

Personal attacks or hostile behavior are not acceptable substitutes for technical discussion.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through the contact method documented in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md).

Security issues should instead follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action in response to violations, including:

- Requesting behavior changes.
- Removing inappropriate content.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation where necessary.

Actions should be proportionate to the situation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and community interactions related to Nexus, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Community channels
- Project events
- Other spaces where participants represent the project

## Good-Faith Participation

The goal of enforcement is to protect the community and maintain a productive environment.

Participants are encouraged to resolve disagreements constructively whenever possible.

## Changes

This Code of Conduct may be updated as the project and community evolve.
